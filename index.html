Questions

Before you join

How does a month work?

Choose up to as many pieces as your plan holds. They are delivered to your door, and you wear each one for thirty days.

When can I swap?

From day two. Pick the next piece in the app and we collect one and bring the other.

Can I keep a piece longer?

Yes. In the last seven days of a piece's month, Extend +30 appears in the app and gives you another thirty days.

What does it cost?

AED 299 a month for one piece at a time, AED 450 for two and AED 599 for three.

Who can join, and where do you deliver?

Membership is by application. You verify your identity in the app, we review it, and once you are approved we deliver in Dubai at a slot you choose.
